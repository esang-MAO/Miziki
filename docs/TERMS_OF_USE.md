## What Miziki is

Miziki is a local-first music player: it plays FLAC, WAV, ALAC and MP3
files you choose from your own device. Your music is never uploaded.
Signing in adds optional features on top — a private backup of your
listening history and settings, and a social layer for sharing your
crate with friends — but the player works fully without ever signing in.

## Who can use it

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. You must own, or
otherwise have the legal right to play, every piece of music you add to
your library. Miziki doesn't provide or sell music — it only plays files
you already have.

## Your account

You're responsible for your own account and for keeping your sign-in
code private (there's no password — codes are single-use and emailed to
you). You can sign out of one device or every device at once from the
Account screen, and delete your account entirely at any time; see the
Privacy Policy for exactly what deletion removes.

## Your content

You own the music, photos, and text (track edits, notes, bio) you add.
By choosing to share something — a crate pick, a note, your now-spinning
status — you're allowing the friends your visibility setting permits to
see it. Miziki doesn't claim ownership of anything you create or upload,
and doesn't use it for any purpose beyond showing it to you and whoever
you've chosen to share it with.

## Community rules

Using the social features, you agree not to:

- impersonate another person or account,
- harass, threaten, or abuse other users,
- post illegal content, or
- upload a profile picture, bio, note, or custom cover that violates any
  of the above.

Reports against other accounts are reviewed. A violation of these rules
can lead to content being removed, an account being restricted, or an
account being deleted, at Miziki's discretion.
