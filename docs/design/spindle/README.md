# Spindle: design handoff

The frames behind BD14a (`docs/38_BD14a_SPINDLE.md`): the Miziki app icon. Same format as `docs/design/launch-intro/`. The `.dc.html` files are the design canvas's source ("Miziki Fermata Mark") and won't render opened directly in a browser; flat PNGs of each are in the handoff's `frames/`.

| Artboard | File |
|---|---|
| Six fermata directions (A Ring hold, B Sundown, C Rising record, D Spindle, E Held i, F Tilted) | `Main.dc.html` |
| Spindle with the unchanged wordmark: horizontal, stacked, light, F cut; Spindle's three size cuts, dark and light | `Lockups.dc.html` |
| Spindle on the home screen beside today's icon, in a 16 px tab, on a paper label with a dead-wax etch, in a back-cover footer | `InUse.dc.html` |

Decided: D, Spindle, is the icon. The wordmark stays the A cut with three spindle rings, and the F cut stays for the launch intro and empty sleeves. Spindle switches cuts by rendered size: full above 40 px, mid from 29 to 40 px, label-only below 29 px. Folding the fermata into the wordmark (the arc across the outer i's with the center ring as its dot) was tried and dropped. On `Main.dc.html`, D's size ladder predates the size cuts and shows why they were needed.
