The fermata on records with no art

Five ways to bring the ochre fermata icon onto the sleeve a record gets when it has no artwork, next to today's. Each at 220, 96 and 48 px. The sticker titles are sample text; the artist is a placeholder.

Today

The dark brown sleeve with the tilted wordmark, as it ships.

A · Stamp

Today's sleeve with the icon as a small label stamp in the corner, like a record company's logo.

B · House sleeve

The icon becomes the sleeve: ochre, a white fermata, and a hand-lettered sticker with the title and artist.

Blue Hour[Artist]

C · Die-cut

The whole fermata is cut out of the ochre sleeve, so the record's own pressing shows through the arch and the dot.

Blue Hour[Artist]

D · Embossed

Today's brown sleeve with the fermata blind-embossed, tone on tone, and the same sticker.

Blue Hour[Artist]

E · Friend's card

For a friend's record with no art: today's pressing-colour title card with a faint fermata.
