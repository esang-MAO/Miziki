One record, four ages

Wear comes from plays and time. It never resets, and there's no restore.
First spin 0 PLAYS Sharp corners, full color, clean edges, a bright label.
A month in 30 PLAYS Edges just starting to scuff, one small corner ding.
A year in 150 PLAYS Sun-fade creeping in from one side, a crease, scuffed edges, the bottom seam starting to open. Hairlines on the vinyl, the label losing ink.
Well loved 500 PLAYS Yellowed toward cream, a folded corner, an old sticker mark, tape over the split seam, a pale label. The sound stays clean.
