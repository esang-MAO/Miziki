Just the cover

The whole icon is the orange sleeve: no record, no label, the white fermata filling the square. To anyone who reads music it's a fermata; to everyone else it can pass for an m. One drawing at every size, so no size cuts.

A · classic fermata, amber #E9A24A

The engraved fermata from the sleeve, scaled up. Reads as a fermata first; the m is a second read.

B · leans m, amber #E9A24A

The arc’s legs run down to a baseline and the dot stretches into a short rounded stem, so the m reads first. The fermata is still there, but less exact.
