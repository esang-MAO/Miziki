Miziki · icon study

Sleeve above, label below

The sleeve and record is the icon wherever there's room for it: the home screen, the App Store, install prompts (40 px and up). Below that, in browser tabs, settings lists and notifications, it hands over to the label alone: the record's orange label with the fermata, its dot the spindle hole. Two ways to colour the small one: Spindle's dark ink, or white to match the sleeve's fermata.
