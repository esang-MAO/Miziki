# Fermata icon: design handoff

The frames behind BD14a (`docs/38_BD14a_FERMATA_ICON.md`): the Miziki app icon. Same format as `docs/design/launch-intro/`. The `.dc.html` files are the design canvas's source ("Miziki Fermata Mark") and won't render opened directly in a browser; flat PNGs of each are in the handoff's `frames/`.

| Artboard | File | Status |
|---|---|---|
| App icon, decided: the icon, app sizes, dark and light home screens, 16 px tab, beside the wordmark | `Final.dc.html` | **Decided** |
| Arch height × dot position, 3×3 | `Shape.dc.html` | Picked: taller arch, dot just below the ends (then weighted) |
| Ochre, burnt orange and bright orange on busy home screens | `Colour.dc.html` | Picked: ochre |
| The same three oranges with a black fermata | `Black.dc.html` | Dropped: black read as an eye |

| Six fermata directions (A Ring hold, B Sundown, C Rising record, D Spindle, E Held i, F Tilted) | `Main.dc.html` | Explored |
| Spindle with the wordmark, and its three size cuts | `Lockups.dc.html` | Dropped |
| Spindle in use: home screen, tab, paper label, back cover | `InUse.dc.html` | Dropped (placements are ideas for after the freeze) |
| The sleeve with the record half out | `Sleeve.dc.html` | Dropped |
| Sleeve from 40 px up, the label below 40 px | `Hybrid.dc.html` | Dropped |

Decided: a full-bleed `#C47F1E` square with a white fermata (taller, weighted arch; dot just below the arch's ends), one drawing at every size. The wordmark stays the A cut with three spindle rings; the F cut stays for the launch intro and empty sleeves. Also tried and dropped, with no frames kept: the fermata folded into the wordmark (an arc across the outer i's with the center ring as its dot).
