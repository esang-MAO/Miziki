The amber ramp · one accent, three steps

tint #F8E2C2
amber #FF9E2C
shade #C47F1E

Dark mode has had the hue and the pale tint but nothing between. The shade (5.4:1 on the sky) takes pressed states, divider lettering on dark, the awning's deeper stripe and inactive accent glyphs, so amber stops being the only warm note. Light mode's shade stays `#9A4700`.

Four secondaries, all shades

Prussian #2B5586
Verdigris #3F8C7E
Puce #7E555A
Olive #6B6A3A

For chips, dividers, rules and the awning: never at full saturation beside amber. The same four are record colours, so the chrome and the pressings share one vocabulary. Olive is the bridge: it sits in both the orange and the purple families, and it is what amber turns into when laid thin over black.

Three rules

  • Amber never signals state: not new, not error, not selected fills. Identity only. State uses the sticker kit or the secondaries.
  • No second warm in the chrome. Burnt orange, harvest gold and chrome-yellow gold stay inside era tints and pressings; together they are the 1970s den.
  • No mauve as a neutral, and no amber in a three-colour stripe with white and green or white and blue.

Paper is a colour you pick, never one you derive by opacity. The bin is cardboard and walnut, not red-brown, so Oxblood and Umber records separate from it. The plinth is darker than any record body so even Jet lifts off it.

Light theme · three moves

ink #26202C
muted #6F665C
accent-text #9A4700

Warm the ink from blue-black to aubergine-black (14:1), replace the cool grey with a warm grey that passes 4.5:1 on cream, and let the light phase anchors lean into their hue (Golden `#F8E2C2`, Dawn `#F7E1DE`) instead of all reading as one cream.

Sky anchors, confirmed

midday
golden · umber

The cooler Golden and Twilight are raw umber and puce: glazes over the same grey ground rather than different colours. Amber reads at 6.7–7.2:1 across the whole arc. Golden's phase chip warms to `#F2B35C` so it is not amber's twin.

Era tints · a decade divider or sticker stripe

Applied as a 20–25% tint over divider stock plus a full-strength 6 px edge stripe; lettering stays aubergine. Warm oranges are avoided in every decade so the tint never competes with the accent. The 1970s row leans avocado and tortoise because the app's own chrome is already the decade's gold.
