Breaking the seal

Rest. Film, strip, tag. The platter stays empty; the sleeve pull won't start until the seal is gone.
Tear. Drag the tab leftward (or tap Play): the strip peels along the perforation, 380 ms, with a 60 ms stagger on the film behind it.
Lift. The film slides up and off in 420 ms, contrast returns to the cover, the tag goes with it. Then the ordinary sleeve pull.
NEW Opened. Full-contrast art and, for 14 days, the butter NEW tag. Reduced motion: film fades in 150 ms, no peel.

The sealed sheet keeps its layout; its header cover uses this same film at 64 px, and its footer line reads "Still sealed. Play breaks the seal." The SEALED text chip in the catalog card becomes the cream tag at 60%.
