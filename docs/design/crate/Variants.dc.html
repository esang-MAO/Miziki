Light banding. Inside a tier the album hash picks a band first, then a colour in it: common 60% of draws, uncommon 30%, scarce 10%. Solids: 20 common, 14 uncommon, 6 scarce (about 1 in 60 lossless albums each). Effects: 16 / 11 / 5. Scarce and uncommon are named under each swatch; the same album still presses the same everywhere. Swirls are two or three feathered streaks, never a wheel. Splatters are small, dense, one ink. Marbles carry veins of the second ink. "Ring" pressings are one colour inside another. Rare pressings come from sessions (the existing rare unlock), not from the file. Tier-1 Jet can take a paper label (#EFE9DF, olive ring #3E3D22) behind Settings › Playback, off by default.

The draw is hashed from the album alone, as today, so a record presses the same in every crate that holds it: you and Ken both have the Verdigris. With 40 solids and 32 effects, two different records rarely share a colour. Only the file changes the draw: Maya's lossy copy is Jet, and that says so without a word.
